## Abstract
We study a martingale problem formulation of a non-instantaneous integrate-and-fire model for a network of interacting biological neurons with uniform synaptic weights, in which the discharges and increments are regularized stochastically.
For this stochastic model we establish solution existence, albeit with a few small technical caveats which we believe to be solvable.
We prove the existence of a (narrow) mean-field limit as the number of neurons goes to infinity.
